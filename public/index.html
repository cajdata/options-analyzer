Select a contract to see its numbers, the stock's earnings history and its recent news.

Contracts are ranked by fixed, visible rules: liquidity, distance to breakeven, how volatility is priced, time decay or yield, and delta. The risk and reward rating compares each price with the stock's own history. Neither is a forecast, and neither is financial advice. This app reads market data. It sends an order only to a paper account, with pretend money, and only the one you have written out and confirmed. It cannot place a real one.
